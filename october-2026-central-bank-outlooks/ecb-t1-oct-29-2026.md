## Notes (declared)

- **Hold at exactly 100% is a defect, not a confident call.** An honest forecast never puts zero probability on a move. The probability
  comes from the model's "course" reading: after a run of holds, it treats the course as settled. The daily outlooks since T-7 have read the
  same. This near-certainty is a known open issue; a fix tested on 2026-10-27 failed its gate. The seal stands as sealed.
- **The comparison arm (G) was not made.** It reads the press through consensus graphs, and those graphs are rebuilt only for banks whose
  meeting calendar is fully captured. Today that is the FOMC only. The arm was refused at the due pass and recorded as missed.
- Everything consumed was knowable at the cutoff. Scoring is by direction against the announced decision.
